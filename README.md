# XOXO Lego Models

Part of a limited merch release to celebrate the launch of [XOXO Explore](https://xoxofest.com/blog/2026-launching-xoxo-explore/), an online archive documenting the history of XOXO and the eight festivals held between 2012 and 2024.

Instructions and BrickLink Studio model files are shared below. Both models are also listed on BrickLink Studio Gallery with Easy Buy enabled, which lets you order all the bricks you need in a single checkout.

Original model designs by Portland designer Nate Funk.

## Washington High Model

<img src="Washington%20High%20Lego%20Set/Washington%20High%20Render%201.jpg" alt="Washington High Lego Model" width="600">

The set is based on the layout of XOXO 2024, featuring our venue, Washington High, our entrance structure, outdoor tent, food carts, and picnic tables. The rear of the model opens to reveal a microfigure conference speaker standing in front of a custom printed XOXO Explore logo tile.

## Explore Logo Model

<img src="Explore%20Logo%20Lego%20Set/Explore%20Logo%20Render%201.jpg" alt="Explore Logo Lego Model" width="600">

The set features our “XOXO Explore” logo, designed by Jen and Richard from [Skinny Ships](https://www.skinnyships.com/), and is made of 324 1x1 tiles in all eight festival year colors.
